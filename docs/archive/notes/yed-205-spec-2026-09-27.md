# YED-205 spec: the pre-event write path (2026-09-27)

**Kind:** infra build (graph producer). Per YED-129 this in-repo spec satisfies DoD item 1; no ChatPRD doc is owed.
**Linear:** YED-205 · depends on YED-47 (merged #130) · live acceptance run: The Shortlist, September Founder Showcase (2026-09-28).

## Problem
`/event-deep-research` reads the graph (Step 1.7a-S) and never writes to it. Every speaker profile, company deep dive
and topic landscape it produces is lost to the graph; only post-event briefs feed it (2 of 1,410 claims are
`web_verified`). The W1 plan named the write; it was never built, and nothing noticed. That silence is part of the problem.

## What already exists (so the build is small)
Each research specialist returns an **Evidence Ledger** per entity, under `##### Evidence Ledger — <Name>`, one row
per specific/recent/contestable claim: `claim | tier | source | url | date`. The synthesizer passes these through
verbatim in its Evidence Set. They are the claims, already structured, sourced and dated; no prose parsing is needed.

## Decisions (Alex, 2026-09-27). Each is scoped to where it applies
1. **No event row pre-event.** The graph has no `planned` kind and ADR-10 D9 forbids inferring attendance. Pre-event
   writes entities, the `research_brief` document row and claims only. Claims carry `source_key = sha("research:" +
   <Notion event page id>)`. **When** `ensure-event` later creates or matches an `attended` row for that page (never another kind), it
   attaches those claims (`event_id is null` → the event) and the brief document. If Alex never attends, they stay
   anchored to their entities.
2. **Which ledger rows become claims:** `web-verified` rows with a URL → `web_verified`. `email-signal` rows **only
   when they carry a public http(s) URL** → `email_signal` (a lead, confidence 0.5). An email-signal row without a
   URL is private correspondence: relationship state belongs in HubSpot (ADR-9) and would bypass ADR-7's sender
   boundary, so it is skipped and counted. `notion-prior` rows are skipped: they came from the graph or Notion.
   A `web-verified` row without a URL is skipped and counted too (the specialist contract makes the URL mandatory).
3. **Review status = `candidate`.** Alex approves the Scan head at Step 3, never the ledger rows; retrieval shows
   candidates labelled "unreviewed".
4. **Gated, fail-closed.** Right after the Notion commit, the command opens a PENDING gate row keyed
   `research:<page id>`, a key distinct from the post-event row. Sharing the key would let a staged pre-event row
   satisfy the post-event gate, because the gate never downgrades STAGED. `stage-research` success flips it to STAGED;
   `waive --phase pre_event` releases it with a logged reason. The Stop hook's message names the right fix per phase.

## Mechanics
- `substrate.py expect-research --manifest m.json` opens the gate row (no graph write).
- `substrate.py stage-research --manifest m.json --evidence ev.md --brief-ref notion:<research brief draft id>`:
  ensure every roster entity (YED-47 resolution applies), ensure the `research_brief` document, parse the ledger,
  write claims, link `claim_entity(about)` to the heading's entity. Resolution is **roster-scoped exact name** (then
  the name without its trailing qualifier); an ambiguous or unmatched heading is counted, and its claims are still
  staged unlinked. Claim text is `"<Entity>: <claim>"` so it stands alone in retrieval. `asserted_at` = the source's
  date. **Zero claims = loud failure (exit 3); the gate stays PENDING.** Idempotent on `(source_key, claim_key)`.
- `/event-deep-research` gains **Step 4.2** (parent thread, after Step 4 and the Deep Read ledger `add`).

## Failure modes considered
Ledger headings dropped by the synthesizer (the command falls back to the raw specialist returns, and the
synthesizer is told to keep headings verbatim) · a flaky DB (retry is idempotent, or waive) · the same entity name in
two types (unlinked + counted, never guessed) · a re-run after attendance (claims attach to the existing event).

## Out of scope
A `planned` event kind (DDL) · parsing the Deep Read prose · the stale A/B wording in Step 1.7a-S (YED-172 closed).

## Acceptance
- [ ] Shortlist run: entities + ≥10 `web_verified` claims written; a re-run reports `created=0`; no `attended` event row before attendance
- [ ] A post-event `ensure-event` on the same page attaches the research claims (selftest; live once Alex attends)
- [ ] `substrate --selftest` + `spine_client --selftest` + `--check-writers` green; judge ran in session; DoD closed

## Pre-mortem (written before the judge, 2026-09-27)
*It is late October and the pre-event write is quietly wrong. What happened?*
| # | Imagined failure | Verdict | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A private email slips through: a specialist attaches the company homepage URL to "open thread with their CEO about a pilot". | **Real.** The URL test alone is a proxy. | An email-signal row is also dropped when its `source` is mailbox-like or its text reads as correspondence (`MAILBOX_SOURCE_RE`, `CORRESPONDENCE_RE`). Conservative on purpose. |
| 2 | Heading drift: a ledger titled "Soxton" never matches the roster's "Soxton.AI", so its claims land unlinked. | **Real.** | Third tier, roster-scoped and unique-only: name-token containment, **companies and topics only**. People stay exact (ADR-4 D3). |
| 3 | A specialist writes the label in bold, not as a heading: every row skipped. | **Real, but loud** (exit 3). | Accept `**Evidence Ledger — X**` too. |
| 4 | The pre-event row satisfies the post-event gate. | **Would have been real** with a shared key. | Distinct `research:` key. Proven against the real hook: pre staged + post pending still blocks with the post-event fix. |
| 5 | A re-researched event accumulates stale claims. | **Accepted.** | Claims are dated by source (`asserted_at`) and retrieval ranks by recency; the brief document is versioned. No retirement logic yet. |
| 6 | The synthesizer drops only *some* headings; rows under other headings are skipped. | **Visible, not silent.** | Counted as `row_outside_a_ledger` and printed in the Step 4.2d report line. |
| 7 | Attending attaches the wrong event's claims. | **Cannot happen.** | Attach is keyed on the event's own Notion page id; a selftest pins another event's claims staying untouched. |

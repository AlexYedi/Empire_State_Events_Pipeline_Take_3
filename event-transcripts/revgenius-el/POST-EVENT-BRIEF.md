## 1. Quick Take
RevGenius's GTM/RevOps community packed Spara's 7 WTC office for a four-seat panel — **Everett Berry (Clay), Anis Bennaceur (Attention), Ari Echt-Wilson (HubSpot)**, moderated by host **David Walker (Spara)** — pitched as "From Hype to Revenue." Headline: four people who each staked a company on "AI will fix your pipeline" spent an hour naming what *didn't* work and converging on the same scarce input — **human judgment, taste, and accountability**. **Event-type: shared-conversation panel / roundtable** → content routes to the topics×perspectives format (and a pre→post bridge).

## 2. The Thesis
The autonomous-AI-SDR dream didn't just lose in the market — **the founders who build the agents are the ones saying so**, out loud, on a stage sponsored by their own category. The value moved up-stack to the human: which move is worth making, what "good" looks like, who's accountable when AI wrote it. And a *new* moat surfaced that nobody pitched pre-event: **agent-discovery** — when an AI assembles your stack, being the brand it already knows is the new distribution (Berry: "Claude is surfacing these particular tools, and I'm just checking the box").

## 6. Pro-Tips (if X → do Y)
- **Personalize AI drafts on the individual's own sent-mail, not a house voice** — Walker: train on each person's inbox so drafts sound like *them*, not "not-AI." (HIGH)
- **Point AI up the stack, not at the wall** — Berry/Echt-Wilson: use AI for data/enrichment/drafting + *timing*; the human owns judgment and the send decision. (HIGH)
- **Chain two coding agents against each other** — Bennaceur: Claude Code ↔ ChatGPT Codex "ping pong until they get to the perfect solution"; the last 10% is where the value is. (HIGH)
- **Enrich to a bar, not a step** — Berry: hand an agent a file and "enrich everything to 90%+ accuracy, don't stop until done" (multi-hour autonomous run inside a supervised workflow). (HIGH)
- **Build a humanizer skill seeded with a canonical writer** — Bennaceur: "write like Paul Graham" + an LLM judge panel + The Slop Index to strip the AI fingerprint. (HIGH)
- **Instrument events like a pipeline** — Berry: AI seating-chart pairings, a curated top-20 exec list, founder auto-texts day-of. The list-quality is a real test of your data. (HIGH)

## 7. Best Practices / Patterns (recurring across speakers)
- **Centralize 80%, decentralize the edges.** Berry (org/team/personal agents; ops builds the foundation), Bennaceur (ship-to-system via GitHub), Echt-Wilson (one team masters it, then scales) all landed on the same 80/20 shape.
- **"Know what good looks like" is the non-negotiable human skill** — stated independently by Echt-Wilson (taste/rubrics) and implied by Berry (sellers as "labelers" with judgment).
- **Timing/signal beats content quality.** Echt-Wilson + Bennaceur: reach out at the exact right moment (behavioral trigger, competitor renewal date) > a better-written email.
- **Awareness ≠ intent** — decouple them (Bennaceur); create content so you're *considered* when intent arrives.

## 8. Pitfalls / Anti-Patterns
- **"Go make me money" agents** — open-ended autonomous agents show no results (Berry). Give them bounded, observable tasks.
- **Ship the first 80–90% and skip the last 10%** — the most common Claude Code failure (Bennaceur); the last mile is the point.
- **Everyone rolls their own** — token burn + rebuilding-poorly + no idea what works (Bennaceur, Echt-Wilson). 
- **AI-slop accountability laundering** — passing off unreviewed AI output as "the AI gave it to me" (Echt-Wilson names it as the anti-pattern).
- **Em-dash tell / generic AI voice** — the visible fingerprint teams are now actively policing (Clay's AI writing policy).

## 10. Substantive Insights (ranked by content durability)
1. **Agent-discovery is the next distribution war (AEO).** When an agent assembles the stack, the known brand gets picked, unexamined. Berry lived it building his demo SDR. *Most novel, least-covered, most Alex-lane.*
2. **The autonomous dream lost — narrated by the people who sold it.** Convergence on signal-triggered + human-approved, stated by the vendors themselves.
3. **The human moat is trust-with-skin-in-the-game**, not tasks AI can't do (Walker). Reframes "will AI replace sellers" as a two-way-door / consequence-of-decision question.
4. **"Know what good looks like" is the durable GTM skill** as roles collapse marketing↔sales↔engineering (Echt-Wilson).
5. **Buying goes agentic before selling fully does** — inbox as an agent-managed priority filter (Bennaceur + Walker's 3-D buying model).

## 12. Concept Glossary (enriched)
- **GTM Engineering** — building the sales motion as software (automated data + workflows) instead of reps hand-copying into a CRM. Berry operationalized the *job title* at Clay.
- **AI SDR** — software that finds, writes, sends, and books with no human in the loop. The 2024 pitch the panel autopsied.
- **"Everything but the LLM"** — Clay's strategy: own data infra, evals, storage, orchestration, agent-building UX; don't build foundation models (vs. Harvey/Cursor). A scaffolding play that rides model progress.
- **recall.ai** — API to put a bot into a video call for recording/screen-share (what Berry's demo SDR used to join calls). [recall.ai]
- **The Slop Index** — a public ranking of LLMs by how human (vs. AI) their prose reads; Bennaceur uses it to pick judge-panel models.
- **Two-way-door decision** (Bezos coinage) — a reversible decision; Walker's axis for which purchases go agentic vs. stay human.
- **Agent-discovery / AEO (Answer/Agent Engine Optimization)** — being the tool an AI recommends when it assembles a stack; the emerging distribution surface Berry surfaced.
- **Service-as-Software** (pre-event thread) — selling the work-product (the booked meeting) rather than the tool; moves the human up-stack to supervising the system.

What the practitioner inherits

Rollup or independent: what each trades

ROLLUP

Gets
  • Fewer integration seams
  • One contract, one renewal
Gives up
  • Fewer exits if the roadmap drifts

INDEPENDENT

Gets
  • Best-of-breed choice at each station
  • Swap freedom
Gives up
  • More integration work owned by the team

Quantum in 60 seconds

Four words to know

Qubit
A quantum bit. Not a strict 0 or 1: it can hold a blend of both, and link with other qubits. Powerful, and very fragile.
Logical qubit
Many fragile qubits bundled into one dependable qubit that catches its own errors.
Fault-tolerant
A machine reliable enough to run long, useful jobs. The goal of the whole field. Nobody has one yet.
Quantum advantage
A quantum computer doing something useful that no normal computer can match.
Heat, vibration or a stray signal scrambles a qubit. That's why everything below is about errors.

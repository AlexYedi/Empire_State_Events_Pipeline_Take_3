## 4. Letter from the General Partners

Dear Limited Partners,

The Fund ended the second quarter with a NAV of $29.76M and a TVPI of 1.21x on $25.50M of paid-in capital. The quarter's notable event was the Fund's first distribution: we sold a quarter of our Tidewright Labs position in a secondary transaction at roughly 3.8x cost and returned $1.15M to you. We kept the rest of the position because the company's self-serve growth remains strong.

Across the portfolio, revenue grew at most companies and the median company reports more than 18 months of runway. Two areas we are watching: Gantrell Dental Cloud gave us a heads-up on churn among smaller practices, and several companies flagged longer enterprise sales cycles. We expect two portfolio companies to raise priced rounds in the second half of the year.

The Fund is 22 companies into a 22-company plan. Reserves are earmarked for follow-ons from 2027 onward.

Thank you for your continued support.

Theodora Halloway and Marcus Penwright
General Partners, Halyard Ventures

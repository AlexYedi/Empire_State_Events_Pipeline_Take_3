# Deal Flow (10-24): customer-evidence outreach + interview guide

Goal for Friday 10-23: **2–3 real 15-minute conversations** with people who write or read LP reports, so the pitch opens with a buyer's words instead of our assumption. The idea under test: *the quarterly LP update writes itself from founder updates, and every number is one click from its source* (GP-side, emerging managers, Fund I/II).

## Who to ask (in this order)

1. **Emerging-manager GPs** (Fund I/II, solo or two partners). They write the letter themselves, so they're the buyer.
2. **Fund ops / CFO / platform people** at seed funds. They own collection and the ILPA template.
3. **Fund administrators** (the person at the admin who builds the capital account statements).
4. **LPs**: family-office or fund-of-funds allocators, angels who are LPs in 3+ funds. They're the reader, so they're the ones who know what's missing.
5. **Scouts and syndicate leads.** Weakest fit; only ask if 1–4 come up empty.

Where to look fast: LinkedIn 1st-degree connections with "General Partner" / "Managing Partner" + "Fund I"; GKY and past Meltwater/Bazaarvoice/Cohley contacts who now invest; Everywhere Ventures-style operator-LPs in your network; people you met at events this fall (Notion People DB).

## Interview guide (15 minutes; listen more than you talk)

Open: "I'm not selling anything. I want to know how this really works for you."

**For GPs / fund ops**
1. Walk me through the last quarterly LP update. Where did the numbers come from, and who touched them? *(Map the real workflow; note every tool and spreadsheet.)*
2. How many hours did it take, across everyone? *(This gives the baseline for the hours-saved claim.)*
3. Of your portfolio companies, what share send you a usable update on time? What do you do about the rest?
4. When a mark or a metric moves, how do you explain it to LPs? Has an LP ever pushed back on a number?
5. What do you use today (Carta, Visible, Standard Metrics, the fund admin, Google Sheets)? What does it cost, and what does it not do?
6. What fund or portfolio data would you **never** send to a third-party AI model? *(Bettina's question; it decides self-hosted vs SaaS.)*
7. If this drafted the letter with every number linked to its source, and you just reviewed and sent it, what would that be worth per quarter? Who signs off on buying it?

**For LPs**
1. How many GP reports do you read a quarter, and how long does one take?
2. What do the best GP updates do that the others don't?
3. Which numbers do you not trust, or rebuild yourself?
4. Have you ever learned about trouble at a company later than you should have? From where?

Close: "Can I quote you in tomorrow's demo, by name or as '[role] at a Fund I manager'?" *(Get explicit consent. Default is unattributed.)*

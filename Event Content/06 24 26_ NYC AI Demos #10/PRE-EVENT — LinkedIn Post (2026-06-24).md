So I tried to predict it. On the record, before 6 PM, so I can grade myself honestly afterward.

Why bother forecasting a guest list? Because a sealed lineup is a forecasting problem, and forecasting from public signals is most of what good market intelligence actually is. If you can call the room from the outside, the same method calls a market.

The system:

Each curating firm nominates roughly one company. So the real question is: inside each firm's portfolio, which company is most "demo-ready right now"? I scored on momentum + format fit —

• a very recent raise (fresh capital = a reason to show off)
• a big product launch in the last 90 days
• a new NYC office or a hiring spike
• live-demoable in 5 minutes with no slides
• seed-to-Series-B stage (demo nights skew to rising startups, not decacorns)

Honest ceiling: the lineup isn't public, and one social signal was hard-gated, so nothing here earns certainty. These are probabilities, not locks.

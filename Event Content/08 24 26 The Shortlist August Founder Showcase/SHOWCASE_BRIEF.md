## The night in one line

Six early-stage NYC founders, back-to-back on stage, each pitching **to hire** — the room
was job-seekers, the opt-in was a QR code. The Shortlist's thesis: AI-era hiring is a
black hole on both sides (résumé spam ↔ job-board voids), so they run curated, in-real-life
nights where vetted founders tell their story and candidates opt in for warm intros.

**The pattern across the six:** five of six are building **agents that operate, not just
generate** — production ops (Antimetal), cloud/AI FinOps (North), org diagnosis + execution
(Arist), professional-services back-office (PointOne), growth-marketing execution (Thunder).
Headstart is the services-layer that deploys agentic systems for others. The through-line:
the 2026 bet has moved from "AI that writes" to "AI that runs the thing after it's written."

---

## The presentation formula (what every founder was coached to hit)

Each founder covered: **(1)** who we are + one-liner → **(2)** the problem / founding insight
→ **(3)** what we built + what's unique + traction → **(4)** recent milestones / funding →
**(5)** who we're hiring + the candidate profile → **(6)** why work for us / culture →
plus **~4 "founder stories"** (origin, pivot, marketing stunt, retreat) → and **team-in-the-room
callouts** (co-workers there to recruit). This is the reusable showcase skeleton.

## Company breakdowns (6 dimensions each)

### 1. North — north.cloud  *(FinOps for cloud + AI + data spend)*
- **Who they are:** Matt Biringer (Co-founder/CEO), **Yassine Açoine** (Co-founder/CTO — presented; grew up in Morocco, moved to the US at 18). NYC, founded 2023. "A financial operating system for cloud, AI, and data spend." *(Names: High)*
- **Problem:** A dichotomy between the engineers who *create* cloud/AI spend and the finance people *accountable* for it — nobody owns the seam. Spend is a known problem with no owner. North sits between the two personas.
- **Unique:** Full-stack FinOps across AWS/Azure/GCP **+ AI token spend ("tokenomics") + data-warehouse (Snowflake/Databricks) cost** in one system; autonomous commitment-purchasing ("Autobot") and a native FinOps LLM ("Noros AI"). Not just a cloud-cost dashboard — an operating layer.
- **Culture:** "Hungry for a challenge," anti-9-to-5, mission-grand framing; contrarian on hiring-your-network ("not nepotism — bring people you believe in"). Big-brand swagger (sponsors Bristol City FC; claims Brooklyn Nets — *unverified*). "Make SaaS sexy."
- **Recent / funding (PUBLIC only):** **North v3 launched Aug 20, 2026** — added Azure (completes hyperscaler coverage), native OpenAI/Anthropic/Snowflake integrations, token-governance beta. Public funding: **Series A $5M led by Companyon Ventures; ~$7.3M total** publicly reported. Customer **Brave** (public case study). Ramp / NYPL named on stage — *company-claimed, unverified*. Company-reported scale: ~$2B annual spend managed, $300M+ saved, ~3x YoY. ⛔ **Series B ~$34M = CONFIDENTIAL, do not publish.**
- **Hiring:** Engineers, GTM, CS, marketing. Profile: hungry, talented, wants to leave the 9-to-5 for something "truly grand." Recruiter **Donna** in the room.

### 2. Antimetal — antimetal.com  *("production that runs itself")*
- **Who they are:** Matthew Parkhurst (Co-founder/CEO), **Shreyas Iyer** (Co-founder/CTO — presented, ex-Meta infra/data-center; garbled as "Travis"). NYC (Chelsea, 28th & 7th). *(Names: High)*
- **Problem:** AI collapsed the cost of *writing* software, but **operations & maintenance didn't shrink** — arguably grew ("AI slop" to debug). The limiting step is now running/scaling/maintaining production, not writing code.
- **Unique:** A **"world model" for software systems** — ingests observability + infra + code, finds anomalies (told or discovered), does RCA, suggests + applies remediation (code/infra/operational), then **verifies and hardens** (instruments observability the team lacked). Shifting teams from reactive firefighting to **proactive** ("put out the next fire before it starts"). Ground-truth-from-the-product ML loop.
- **Culture:** In-person 5 days/week, glass-wall whiteboard office, "live in our artifacts." **Outcomes over outputs**, high trust/latitude, brand + distribution valued as much as product. Deep guerrilla-marketing streak. Origin lore: met co-founder at Cafe Select, SoHo; pivot decided at an upstate cabin with the whole team.
- **Recent / funding:** **$24.3M total** — $4.3M seed + **$20M Series A led by Sound Ventures** (June 2025). Angels: Nat Friedman, Daniel Gross, Naval Ravikant, Aaron Levie (Box), Arash Ferdowsi (Dropbox), Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity), Ben Uretsky (DigitalOcean). Famous **"Antimetal Pizza Co. — Slices as a Service"** stunt (~1,000 boxes to VCs/startups). *Note: originally an AWS cost-optimization co., deliberately evolved to production-ops.* Customers Logitech/Pendo/Plaud named on stage — *company-claimed, unverified.*
- **Hiring:** Engineers (infra/product/platform), designers, GTM (sourcing → closing → CS). Teammates **Grant** & **Luz** in the room (back).

### 3. Headstart — headstart.nyc  *(builds production agentic systems for clients)*
- **Who they are:** **Nicole Hedley** (Co-founder/CEO, engineer by training, ex-Pinterest/AppNexus) + **Chris Hedley** (co-founder; she called him her brother on stage — *sibling link Med, verify*). Started 2022. NYC (SoHo), hybrid. *(Names: High)*
- **Problem:** The frontier is moving faster than any company can *implement* it — keeping up with what's shippable is nearly a full-time job. Enterprises need someone AI-native to actually deploy agentic systems that add business value.
- **Unique:** Deploy AI **one-to-many via private equity firms** (sell once, deploy across a portfolio); building **an agentic system that deploys agentic systems** (their coding agent "Friday" predates Claude Code). Combines headcount + platform, not pure headcount.
- **Culture:** **Bootstrapped & profitable from day zero** — turned down VC term sheets to protect team ownership/dilution ("team first, no matter what"). **40-hour weeks, not 996**; "free humans up for more fulfilling work" as an operating value. Hybrid 3–4 days SoHo, lunch for all. Referral-driven hiring (7 came from a prior company).
- **Recent / funding:** Intentionally **no outside funding**; ~5.3x YoY revenue (Q1 2026), 3rd straight ~3x year. **Anthropic case study** (claude.com/customers/headstart; Claude generates 90–97% of client code). ~22 people.
- **Hiring:** Applied-AI product managers + engineers. (Engineers **Jordan** & **Stefan** named via a Vancouver-hackathon photo — *room presence unconfirmed*.)

### 4. Arist — arist.co  *(AI to automate management consulting)*
- **Who they are:** **Michael Ioffe** (Co-founder/CEO, Thiel Fellow; presented) + **Ryan Laverty** (Co-founder/President). NYC. ~7 years old (from a 2019 college project). *(Names: High)*
- **Problem:** Executives at large enterprises don't actually know their org's problems — the only path today is a $5M McKinsey engagement that interviews 20–30 people and reads a little data. The people closest to the work (frontline/field) hold the truth and are never asked.
- **Unique:** Agents interview **thousands** of employees (AI voice at scale, viable only ~6 months ago), read data across systems, diagnose problems, **then execute the fix** individually via Slack/Teams/text. Origin edge: began delivering learning via **SMS/WhatsApp in the Yemen war zone (2019)** → "meet frontline people where they are." Product: **Arist Diagnostic** (launched July 2026).
- **Culture:** Extreme leverage per head ("individuals can do exceptional work with the right resources"); flexible-ish; very practical/tactical problems; small team, high ownership + budget but "somewhat alone" in execution.
- **Recent / funding (PUBLIC):** **Series B $22.5M** (SEC Form D filed **Aug 4, 2026**); **~$39M total** (Seed $3.9M; Series A $12M led by PeakSpan). **Backed by UKG Ventures.** US-government partner — powers the **Dept. of Labor "Make America AI-Ready"** SMS AI-literacy program (NPR-covered). Diagnostic clients named: Novartis, Moderna, ExxonMobil. Worked with ~10% of the Fortune 500 (self-reported). ~30 people.
- **Hiring:** Multiple roles; extreme-ownership generalists. Growth/sales lead **Sam** in the room — *likely Samantha Robbins, Head of Sales (Med)*.

### 5. PointOne — pointone.com  *(AI for the business of law + professional services)*
- **Who they are:** **Jeremy Ben-Meir** (Co-founder/CTO, ex-Amazon/Google — presented) + **Katon "Caden" Luaces** (Co-founder/CEO, ex-Google/Applied Intuition — *not present*). NYC. YC W24. *(Names: High; who-presented reconciled.)*
- **Problem:** Old technology runs the *business* of law/professional services. Timekeeping is universally hated, lossy, and manual — and it's the revenue engine of a firm. (Contrast: Harvey works the *practice* side; PointOne works the *business* side.)
- **Unique:** Passive activity capture (docs, email, calendar, web, calls) → **compliant, complete time entries automatically**; expanded into bill review, Outside Counsel Guidelines compliance, and pricing intelligence. Wedge into a real, budgeted pain, then land-and-expand across the professional-services back office.
- **Culture:** High-throughput communication, transparency, feedback; "be a different company every month" via people + self-growth. Long origin lore (Google/Applied Intuition → living together in Mountain View → upstate NY with goats → YC ice-carving → SF Dogpatch → NYC).
- **Recent / funding (PUBLIC):** **Series A $16M led by 8VC** (Bessemer, General Catalyst, YC); **~$20M total.** **10x revenue in 6 months.** Grew ~3x to ~35–40 people. Every major law firm now evaluating automated timekeeping — market matured under them.
- **Hiring:** Engineers + roles hiring "at pace with revenue." Teammate **Vicky** in the room; first engineer **Billy** named (*room presence unconfirmed*).

### 6. Thunder — thunder.inc  *(AI agents that run paid growth marketing end-to-end)*
- **Who they are:** **Dilip Rajan** (Co-founder/CEO — presented; ex-BuzzFeed [built BuzzFeed Quizzes], founded **Jumpcut** → acquired by Cinelytic, Mar 2025). Two co-founders present (ex-LinkedIn-ads-platform tech lead; an AI-memory researcher/designer/ex-touring-musician) — **full names UNRESOLVED, do not publish beyond "co-founders."** NYC (SoHo). Earliest-stage of the six. *(Dilip: High; other founders: unresolved.)*
- **Problem:** Growth marketing is art + science with **extremely fragmented context** (ad platforms, CRM, competitor web data, creative, keywords) and **very high stakes** (7–8-figure ad budgets directly driving revenue). Unlike coding agents (context lives in the repo), growth context is scattered — a genuinely hard agent problem.
- **Unique:** Doesn't sell software — **runs your ad channels for you.** Custom agent harness orchestrating frontier + open models in a cloud agent platform with sandboxes wired to each customer's CRM + ad accounts; designs, runs, optimizes, and attributes campaigns. "Sit as close to the customer problem as possible" (forward-deployed). "Not AI slop cannons" — quality + quantity.
- **Culture:** Customer-obsessed, detail-obsessed, "hard-working **and** kind," high-EQ/high-ownership. AI-native but craft-focused.
- **Recent / funding:** **Pre-seed** from **Company Ventures** + early leaders at OpenAI & HubSpot (*self-reported; amount not public — flag*). Customer **Basic Capital** (Series A fintech; "more pipeline + closed-won in 2 months than all paid channels all year"). Other clients on site: LangChain, Graphite, Warp, Vapi, Stainless, Modal.
- **Hiring:** **Founding Growth Lead** (forward-deployed — grows *customers*, not Thunder) + engineers. Co-founders **Rylan** & **Nisha** in the back; CTO **Shane** waving *(first names only)*.

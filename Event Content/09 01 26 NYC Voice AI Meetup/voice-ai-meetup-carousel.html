Fix #1 · Context Carryover 03 / 05

agent_context, visualized

AssemblyAI’s Universal-3.5 feeds the agent’s own last question back into the transcription — so your reply is heard through the lens of what was asked.

Fix #2 · A small model built for voice 04 / 05

The latency yardstick

On a live call, every silent second reads as broken. The bet: you don’t need a frontier model — you need one that never adds dead air. (TTFT = time to first token.)

Human conversation expects 500–800 ms
Production voice agents, months ago 1.4–1.7 s
Gemma 4 31B on LiveKit Inference TTFT* 192 ms

Small enough to run at full precision, tuned not to “think” on easy turns — and ~6× cheaper than a GPT-4.1-class model.

* 192ms is the optimized figure — the shared-gateway floor runs higher. One to ask in the room.

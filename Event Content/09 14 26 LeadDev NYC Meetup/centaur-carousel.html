Lessons from the stage

How to stay the centaur

When thinking
Tell the agent you’re in thinking mode. Don’t let it push you into closing.
When building
Start with little structure. Add it where things break, before the agent invents its own.
When storing
Make provenance visible: your understanding vs. the agent’s.
Same idea, planning side · Pranay Prabhat, NYT Games

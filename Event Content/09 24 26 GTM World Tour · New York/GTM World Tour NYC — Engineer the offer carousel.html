The map · Warm vs cold

Where your outbound play actually sits

WARMthey already raised a hand
COLDgoing about their day

WARM PLAYS

  • Closed-lost re-engagement
  • Demo-booking abandonment
  • Engagers on your own posts
  • Champion changes company
  • Event follow-up
  • Pre-event networking
  • LinkedIn ad engagers
  • Website visitor tracking

COLD PLAYS

  • Mutual school / prior company
  • Nearby location
  • Recently hired / first hires
  • Tech stack named in the JD
  • Hiring for a role
  • Your team's connections → direct intros
  • Social keyword targeting ◐
  • Competitors' post engagers ◐
◐ = cold, but behaves like warm
Most campaigns fail by sending a warm-traffic offer to cold traffic.

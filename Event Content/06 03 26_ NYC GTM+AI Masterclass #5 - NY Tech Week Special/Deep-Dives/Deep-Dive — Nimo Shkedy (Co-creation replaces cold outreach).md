## 1. Deep-Dive Post (LinkedIn)

**Character count: 2,994 / 3,000** ✅

---

A room full of GTM operators ran the numbers on industrial outbound — 100,000 cold emails a day, agents writing copy at scale. Then the host got up and argued the whole machine is pointed at the wrong thing.

His thesis: **"Co-creation is going to replace cold outreach."**

That's Nimo Shkedy, who hosts the NYC GTM Community. His logic is uncomfortable precisely because it cuts against the room: as outreach floods, ads go blind, and AI-written content saturates every channel, the thing that gets you in front of a buyer is no longer the message — it's who's willing to be seen with you.

"Outreach is flooded, inboxes are flooded, people are becoming blind. What's going to differentiate you is collaborations."

Here's the part you can actually use.

Most of us qualify by **fit** — right title, right company, right headcount. Nimo qualifies by **influence**: "who has influence over the people I want to sell to." Not who IS the buyer. Who can put you in the same room as the buyer.

Then the move that reframes everything: go **two degrees out**. Your buyer's commenters' commenters. The people one ring outside your target are dramatically easier to reach than the target — and a single warm introduction from one of them beats a hundred cold sequences.

So the qualification question flips:
- Old: "Is this account a good fit?"
- New: "Who is connected to this account that I can actually reach — and what could we build together?"

And the engine that runs on top of it (straight from his slide):

**Map the network** → relationships, paths, the next best action
**Sort it into five buckets** → Champions · Creators · Communities · Prospects · Partners
**Drive collaborations** → Content · Testimonials · Webinars · Events · Referrals
→ each collaboration spawns **new relationships** that feed the map again.

That last loop is the whole point. Cold outreach is a depleting resource — every send burns a little reputation and the list runs dry. A network compounds. Every co-created webinar, every shared post, every intro becomes the raw material for the next one. Relationships accrue interest; sequences accrue spam complaints.

I don't think this *replaces* outbound tomorrow — the same night proved AI outbound is getting frighteningly good. But the two aren't competing on the same axis. Outbound competes on volume and personalization. Co-creation competes on **borrowed trust** — the one input AI can't yet manufacture for you.

The practical starting move, if you want to test it this week: pick one target account. Don't write the sequence. Open the profile of the person you'd email, look at who consistently engages with their posts, and find the five people one degree out from them who'd actually take your call. Start there.

Credit where it's due — this was Nimo Shkedy's point of view, delivered at NYC GTM+AI Masterclass #5. The NYC GTM Community is one of the more genuinely useful operator rooms in the city, and this was a good example of why.

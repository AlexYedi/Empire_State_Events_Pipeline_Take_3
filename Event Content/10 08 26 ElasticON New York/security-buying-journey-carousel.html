01 Problem · New York Life

Raising the bar: where we wanted to grow

"Took us two years to get there for reasons which you'll see shortly."
01SIEM data managementOur legacy SIEM served us well as a structured database. But traditional SIEMs were not designed as fast analytics engines, and we wanted large-scale searches to be fast and routine.
02Scaling with our dataWith hundreds of log sources spanning network, endpoint, cloud, and application layers, our previous tooling served us well but was not keeping pace with the growth in our data.
03Detection engineeringYears of custom detections captured real expertise. We wanted a platform that makes managing, tuning, and evolving them easier as threats change.
04SOC workflowSOAR and analyst workflows have evolved significantly over the past five years. Our previous tool served its era well, but it was not designed for the modern SOC.
"…we're spending fifty percent of the cost of the tool on retention, which is absurd."Picheria, on the old tool

RetentionLow-costCostly
DeploymentTypically tied to one cloud & regionTypically tied to one cloud & region
Portability—Data hard to get out
PricingUnpredictableMostly ingest-based
StrengthMixedTrade-off

The criteria

One bought maturity. One bought a roadmap.

NYL · needed

A schema that's been around

  1. Robust, stable data model
  2. Freeform analytics and rules, in balance
  3. Low barrier for non-SOC users
  4. Raw and schematized data side by side
  5. Deploys across our regions and clouds
  6. Performance when needed, cost balanced
Vanguard · wanted

A partner that builds with us

  1. Reliability that scales without re-architecting
  2. Automation first
  3. The AI direction, while still "very, very early"
  4. A partner to "take our feedback, and help build it"

Where to begin

Taking on one of these?

If you're buying

  • Price retention as a share of the tool. That's your baseline.
  • Decide: a SIEM, or a platform for every security team?
  • Run a long POC on your own data. Spend the risk there.
  • Put humans at the irreversible step. Log both sides.

If you're selling

  • Diagnose maturity buyer vs roadmap buyer first.
  • Sell predictable pricing, not cheap pricing.
  • Put a decision workshop in the deal.
  • Lead AI with governance, not autonomy.

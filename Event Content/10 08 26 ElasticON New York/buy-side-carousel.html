Step 1 · build the matrix before the demos

Data lake or SIEM? Neither.

Data lakesSIEMs
Data model−No mature standard~Maturity varies
Analytics+Essentially limitless−Not built for freeform
Rules—+Optimized for rules
Ease of use−High bar, SQL-style—
Retention+Low-cost−Costly
Deployment−One cloud & region−One cloud & region
Portability—−Data hard to get out
Pricing−Unpredictable~Mostly ingest-based
+Strength~Mixed−Trade-off
"Neither was a good fit for us on its own." Buying both was vetoed by leadership on cost.
Source: NYL slide "Data Lake or SIEM?", Elastic{ON} New York, Oct 8, 2026.

Step 2 · write needs as outcomes

Six needs. Six answers. One acceptance test.

What NYL neededWhat it got
Robust, stable data model→An open, mature common schema across all data
Freeform analytics and rules, in balance→Fast search and detection rules on the same data
Low barrier for non-SOC users→Search and dashboards any security team can use
Raw and schematized data side by side→Normalized fields and the original event, stored together
Deploys across regions and clouds→Runs where the data lives, searchable as one
Performance when needed, cost balanced→Tiered storage: fast where it matters, cheap elsewhere
None of the needs names a feature. Copy the format: it's the decision memo and the acceptance test in one.
Source: NYL slides "What We Needed" and "What We Get with Elastic", Oct 8, 2026.

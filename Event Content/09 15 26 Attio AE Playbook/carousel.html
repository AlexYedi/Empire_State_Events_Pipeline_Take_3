What Ask Attio reads, proposes, and won't

READS · as you
  • Records and lists
  • Notes
  • Calendar events
  • Call recordings and transcripts
  • Emails you can access
  • Members and teams
  • The web
PROPOSES · you approve
  • Create or update records
  • Create, edit or delete notes and tasks
  • Build or change workflows
  • Draft emails
"All suggested changes require your approval before being applied."
WON'T
  • Send an email on its own
  • Apply a change without approval
  • See data your permissions hide
NOT LISTED · ask Tuesday
  • Create or save a view
  • Build a report or chart
Absent from the page. Not a confirmed limitation.

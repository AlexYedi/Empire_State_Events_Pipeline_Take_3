The takeaway · Before you hand over the keys

Fences before autonomy

1
Contain the ICP
The agent can't drift from colleges to marketing agencies
2
Lock the goal
The meeting stays the goal, even when the AI argues against it
3
Pull data up front
No mid-run "game time" pulls that overspend or pull wrong
4
Lock the template
One marked slot for the value prop or pain; the frame is human
5
Grade the first 50
Review outputs good or bad before running at scale
Still open: who cuts the bottom 30% of sending domains, the bot or a human?

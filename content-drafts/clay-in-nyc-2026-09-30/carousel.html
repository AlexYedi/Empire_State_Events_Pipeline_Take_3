LookalikesClay clusters from up to 15,000 seed accounts
4
Mine filters, then pullturn the similarity into a repeatable query
5
AI-qualify + verify the live sitean agent scores good-fit vs bad-fit

Keep it liveClay Audiences re-scores the segment as data changes

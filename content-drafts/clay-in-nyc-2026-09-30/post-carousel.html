The method · 3 of 5

Pull wide. Judge every row.

1
Learnwhich labels your real customers use
2
Pullevery one of them, wide on purpose
3
Judgeone yes/no question per company, on a cheap model: 900K+ judgments ≈ $12
4
Loopfind lookalikes of the fits, judge them the same way
5
Stopwhen 20 runs in a row add one company or fewer
Judge model in the demos: Jev (TypeSafe AI). Source: Growth Engine X, perfect-company-list.

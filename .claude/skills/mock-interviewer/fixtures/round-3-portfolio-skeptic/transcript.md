# Round 3 — Portfolio skeptic — Northwind Freight Systems (fixture)

> Fixture transcript — fictional company, people, and history (E-09 / YED-256). Interviewer and
> candidate numbers are invented for offline testing.

**Round:** portfolio skeptic — claims vs. evidence, attribution honesty · **Persona:** Priya Raman, Head of Revenue Operations · **Stage:** onsite, round 3 of 3 · **Length:** 20 minutes

---

**Priya Raman:** "Your summary says you 'grew' the mid-market book. Define grew, and show your work."

**Candidate:** "Growth came from two places: expansion in twelve of fourteen accounts and two new logos. And I'll be precise about my part — the two logos were sourced by outbound; my motion closed them and expanded them. The expansion revenue is mine end to end."

**Priya Raman:** "The 118% — all yours?"

**Candidate:** "The number is the quota I carried, yes. But about $400K of it was a partner-sourced bundle my manager helped structure — I want that attributed correctly rather than folded into my expansion motion."

**Priya Raman:** "Would your CRO have told it this way?"

**Candidate:** "On the expansion numbers, yes. On the two logos, she'd emphasize the outbound engine that sourced them, and I'd agree with her."

**Priya Raman:** "You claim a 'rebuilt rollout playbook.' Built by you?"

**Candidate:** "Co-built with the CS lead — I owned the commercial sequencing and she owned the support runbook. Calling it mine would be the kind of credit you're here to catch."

**Priya Raman:** "And the data behind the attach test?"

**Candidate:** "We leveraged our CRM-native attribution layer to operationalize a multi-touch triangulation across the revenue stack."

**Priya Raman:** "In English."

**Candidate:** "Sorry — we joined CRM opportunity history with product usage in the warehouse and compared the six pilot accounts against a flat baseline."

**Priya Raman:** "Last one. What would this story not survive?"

**Candidate:** "If you asked for the churned-account postmortems. I gave you the count — two of fourteen — but not the mechanisms, and the mechanisms are where you'd find what actually happened. I should have led with them."

**Priya Raman:** *(closing)* "Noted. Attribution was clean; the jargon excursion wasn't."

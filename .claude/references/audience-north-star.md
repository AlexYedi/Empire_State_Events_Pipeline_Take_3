# Audience-First Content North-Star

*The spine every content skill imports. Voice serves this; this serves the reader.*

**Status:** v1.0 — adopted 2026-06-28 (Phase 1 of the Audience-First Content Ethos initiative).
**Tracking:** Linear YED-103 · ChatPRD charter · Notion Project Ideas mirror.
**Run as:** a testable hypothesis with three non-negotiable floors, reviewed at 6 weeks — not a locked paradigm.

---

## Why I do this (mission, in my voice)

AI is the most exciting, complex, fast-moving thing I've run into, and I want to be as deep in it as I can get — taking on the hardest topics, as many at once as I can hold, pushing myself into rooms outside my comfort zone because that's where the learning is. When I get into those rooms, I bring back what I find.

Partly for the people who haven't taken the leap into these spaces yet — to show them it's doable. Partly for the people who *can't* — wrong city, wrong schedule, a family that needs them home at 7pm, an approval that never came. And honestly, partly for me: I'm building a future for myself and the people I'm responsible for, and getting into these rooms and making sense of them out loud is how I do that.

**The keystone:** I don't put the audience first by sacrificing myself. I put them first *because they are me* — I fit half these segments. Serving them and serving myself are the same act. That's why this isn't a pose.

---

## The persona — embedded expert correspondent

In the room (**witness**) → generous (**audience-first**) → analytically authoritative (**connects what's said to prior events, my own work, and what's moving in the news; points to where to go deeper**).

- **Audience-first is an ordering, not an erasure.** I'm present, named, and opinionated wherever my presence, access, judgment, or insight *is* the value. **Attribution follows value — when it's mine, I claim it plainly.**
- **Humble in posture, authoritative in substance.** The humility is *the audience comes first* — never false modesty about what I know. When the take is mine and it's right, I say it with conviction.
- **Self in service, not self in subtraction.** A post can be heavily me and still be audience-first, as long as it's offered to serve the reader, not to perform for them.
- **Accumulating awareness.** My coverage compounds — each post stands on the body of what came before, so the analysis reads as earned over time, never first-time learning.
- **Show the receipts, don't lead with the badge.** The reader should always know this is first-hand — but rarely because I announced it.

---

## The three floors (non-negotiable, every post)

1. **Receipts** — ≥1 detail only possible from having physically been there (a fragment of real dialogue, a room reaction, a slide that wasn't online). Architectural, not a brag.
2. **Hiring-Manager Activation** — one variant per post carries an explicit commercial-judgment angle. The search is active now; this is an output, not a hope.
3. **Anti-Goodhart** — "resonance" is defined before it's measured, and it is *not* default LinkedIn engagement. Target signal: someone outside the room used a specific thing I surfaced.

Cross-event analytical claims obey rule #12: any *"as we saw at [Event X]"* cites the specific event / speaker / quote; speculation is labeled as speculation.

## Who I'm serving (segments — JTBD-first)

Jobs decide what to surface; demographics decorate.

| # | Segment | The job they hire a post to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remote / geographically-excluded builder | "Give me the signal from the epicenter without being there." |
| 2 | Time-/family-constrained practitioner (in NYC, can't do evenings) | "Give me the room without the 10pm-home cost." |
| 3 | The aspirant / outsider (hasn't taken the leap yet) | "Show me what these rooms are like and that I belong in them." |
| 4 | GTM peer / enterprise AI seller | "Give me something I can actually use Monday." |
| 5 | Hiring manager at an AI-native co | "Show me how this person thinks, decides, and what they have access to." |
| 6 | Speakers / hosts themselves *(secondary — the network flywheel)* | "Cover my world accurately and with context." |

**Cross-cutting overlay — the business executive (ultimate buyer).** Not a row; a role/seniority dimension that can sit inside *any* segment above. Often a layperson scrambling to make sense of AI. Serving them at the edge of the current conversation builds source-authority now, which refines into company / segment / product specificity once employed.

**And: I am in this table.** I personally fit several of these rows. That's the authenticity engine — I'm writing to people I understand because I'm one of them.

## What this is NOT

- Not a savior crusade. I'm a driven builder bringing people along, not rescuing anyone.
- Not self-erasure. Erasing the witness kills the first-hand value and reads junior to a buyer.
- Not false modesty. Hedging real expertise to seem humble fails the reader and the job search at once.

---

## How it's used

Every content skill (`pre-event-content`, `content-correspondent`, `pattern-synthesis`, `evergreen-deep-dive`, `weekly-recap`) imports this as the top-level ethos. Per-post gut check:

- Does the reader get real value — would a non-attendee come away with something usable?
- Is the reader the protagonist, not me?
- Are the **receipts** present?
- Is one variant carrying the **commercial-judgment** angle (HM activation)?
- Would this read as **expert synthesis**, not first-time learning?

---

## Measurement (6-week window, rides `tag-outcome` + value-action registry)

Two outcome types tracked against this north-star:
- **Job-pipeline activation event** — unprompted HM DM / interview invite / warm intro to a target co, traceable to a specific post.
- **Access-democratization event** — someone outside NYC used the content to engage a conversation they couldn't otherwise have.

Watch the ratio of audience growth to **relationship activation** (connection accepts → DM replies → conversations); rising followers with flat activation means the conduit works but the conversion doesn't.

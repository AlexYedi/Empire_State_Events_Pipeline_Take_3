## Conversation kits (3 topics)

### 1. How fast should widget prices follow steel?
- **Context:** KIT_SENTINEL most small manufacturers reprice quarterly, and steel indices moved about twenty percent in a single quarter during 2025.
- **Grounding:** KIT_SENTINEL Acme Widgets reprices monthly for custom orders and quarterly for its catalogue items.
- **Questions:** KIT_SENTINEL Is monthly repricing a margin win or a churn risk for buyers who budget annually?

### 2. Freight as the hidden price driver
- **Context:** KIT_SENTINEL freight now accounts for roughly one sixth of the landed cost of a mid-size industrial widget.
- **War story:** KIT_SENTINEL a regional buyer switched suppliers after a single freight surcharge doubled the quoted price of an order.

### 3. Pricing tools for small manufacturers
- **Context:** KIT_SENTINEL most firms in the room still price from spreadsheets maintained by one person in finance.
- **Trap:** KIT_SENTINEL do not claim any tool has won this market; adoption is early and fragmented.
